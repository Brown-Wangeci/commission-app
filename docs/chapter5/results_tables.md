## 12. NFR-01: application load test (measured this session)

**100 concurrent virtual users, non-external-service routes only** (see
`docs/chapter5/environment.md` for why `/api/health` is excluded: it itself
calls the scoring service). Routes: `GET /api/client/escrow`,
`GET /api/admin/review?tab=waiting`, `GET /api/admin/activations/[id]/audit`.

| Route | Requests | mean | p50 | p95 | p99 | max | within 2s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overall (pooled) | 5,761 | 523.6ms | 457.2ms | 1,014.8ms | 1,690.2ms | 1,809.9ms | 5,761/5,761 (100.00%) |
| `GET /api/client/escrow` | 1,975 | 520.3ms | 419.8ms | 1,290.6ms | 1,442.7ms | 1,717.2ms | 1,975/1,975 (100.00%) |
| `GET /api/admin/review?tab=waiting` | 1,894 | 483.2ms | 454.9ms | 1,010.7ms | 1,071.9ms | 1,799.1ms | 1,894/1,894 (100.00%) |
| `GET /api/admin/activations/[id]/audit` | 1,892 | 567.4ms | 475.1ms | 1,670.3ms | 1,731.1ms | 1,809.9ms | 1,892/1,892 (100.00%) |

**Target MET.** 100% of all 5,761 requests at 100 concurrent virtual users
over a 30-second run completed within the 2-second target (overall p95

`docs/chapter5/environment.md`, over loopback with no hosted deployment --
see the caveat above for what this does and does not establish about
agents on mobile data.

## 13. NFR-02: scoring round-trip latency (measured this session)

Real HTTP calls to the running scoring service, one request at a time
(matching how the app's synchronous submission path actually calls it),
built with the same 90-day trailing history window the app sends
(`app/src/lib/scoring/payload.ts::HISTORY_DAYS`).

| Scope | n | mean | p50 | p95 | p99 | max | min | within 1s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overall | 400 | 1.662s | 1.423s | 4.026s | 5.005s | 7.111s | 0.058s | 115/400 (28.75%) |
| t0 mode | 200 | 1.684s | 1.439s | 4.223s | 6.042s | 7.111s | 0.112s | 57/200 (28.50%) |
| t7 mode | 200 | 1.639s | 1.401s | 4.008s | 5.005s | 6.594s | 0.058s | 58/200 (29.00%) |

**Target NOT met.** The 1-second target is missed by a wide margin: mean
round trip is 1.66s, p95 is 4.03s, and only 28.75% of the 400 sampled calls
complete within 1 second. No tuning was applied; these are the real,
observed numbers for the scoring service as the app actually calls it
(90-day trailing history, one request at a time). Sample of n=200
activations per mode, fixed seed 20261109, out of 90,533 total activations
in the dataset -- see the caveat above and `docs/chapter5/environment.md`.
Measured 2026-11-09.

Source: `scripts/measure_scoring_latency.py` (committed); raw output
`docs/chapter5/evidence/performance/nfr02-scoring-latency.json` and
per-call samples in `nfr02-scoring-latency-samples.csv`. Sample, not the full
~90,000-row dataset -- see the script's own docstring and
`docs/chapter5/environment.md` for why. Measured on the same hardware as
table 12; there is no hosted deployment.
